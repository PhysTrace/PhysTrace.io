Generative reconstructions · IntPhys2 Impossible clips

When does the video break physics?

Each clip is noised to σ = 0.8 and reconstructed by a video world model. PhysTrace slides a 2 s window with a 1 s hop over the reconstruction: Qwen3-VL-8B-Thinking writes what it predicts the window should show and the state it actually sees, and PE-AV-Large scores how well the window matches. The three lowest windows (the first window is excluded) form the detected interval around the lowest one. It is a hit when that interval overlaps the annotated violation.

Original videos · IntPhys2 Possible / Impossible pairs

Can it find the violation in a real video?

No generative model is involved here. PhysTrace runs directly on the original IntPhys2 videos. Each scene comes as a Possible video and its Impossible twin, which differs only in the violation. The pipeline is the same: a 2 s window with a 1 s hop, Qwen3-VL-8B-Thinking writes the prediction and the state, and PE-AV-Large scores each window. When the history says an object should be there and the frames disagree, prediction and state split and the score drops. The impossible video falls to a much lower minimum than its possible twin.
