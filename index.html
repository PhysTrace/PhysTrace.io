When does the video break physics?

Each clip is noised to σ = 0.5 or σ = 0.8 and reconstructed by a video world model. PhysTrace slides a 2 s window with a 1 s hop over the reconstruction: Qwen3-VL-8B-Thinking writes what it predicts the window should show and the state it actually sees, and PE-AV-Large scores how well the window matches. The three lowest windows (the first window is excluded) form the detected interval around the lowest one. It is a hit when that interval overlaps the annotated violation.
